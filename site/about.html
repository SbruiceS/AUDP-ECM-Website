About the organisation

Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF)

Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF) is an agriculture research and innovation organisation focused on one problem that every modern farm depends on: data that arrives complete and correct, from the sensor in the field to the decision that uses it.

What ASPF works on

AUDP — ASPF Unified Device Protocol

A protocol family for agricultural devices: sensors, controllers and gateways exchanging small, frequent messages over constrained links.

AUDP-ECM

The error-correction engine of AUDP: detects, corrects and recovers damaged telemetry so soil, climate and equipment data stay trustworthy. Learn more →

Research & innovation

Applied research on reliable farm telemetry, signal quality and data integrity in real agricultural environments. Research areas →

Where it is used

  • Agricultural sensors: soil moisture, temperature, humidity, water and crop monitoring
  • Farm telemetry over radio, long cables and shared or congested channels
  • Distributed field devices, gateways and firmware communication links
  • Constrained networks where retransmission is slow, costly or impossible

Principles

  • Mathematics first — proven coding theory rather than black-box hardware.
  • Correctness you can check — every recovered value is verified before it is used.
  • Safety by design — memory-safe software, strict input validation, continuous testing.
  • Honest scope — error correction protects against noise, not attackers; security layers stay separate.
