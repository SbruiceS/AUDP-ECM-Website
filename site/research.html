Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF)

Agriculture research & innovation

ASPF's research turns the mathematics of reliable communication into practical tools for agriculture, so that precision farming, crop research and automated field systems can rely on the data they collect.

Research areas

Reliable farm telemetry

Rural radio links suffer bursts of interference from weather, machinery and distance. ASPF studies how to spread data so that long bursts remain correctable, and measures the effect under realistic channel conditions.

Sensor signal quality

Receivers often know which values are doubtful. Using that knowledge lets a decoder recover roughly twice as much damaged data, an approach ASPF applies to low-power agricultural sensors.

Channel modelling & simulation

Reproducible simulations of noise, bursts and dropouts in farm environments, used to choose the right amount of protection for each device and link.

Trustworthy agricultural software

Memory-safe implementations, exhaustive and property-based testing, and verified results, so that field data systems fail safely instead of silently.

From research to practice

Research results flow into AUDP-ECM, the error-correction engine of the ASPF Unified Device Protocol, and its Python and D bindings used by agricultural data pipelines.

Work with us

Agriculture research organisations, universities, agritech start-ups and innovation programmes are welcome to get in touch about joint research, field trials or evaluation of AUDP-ECM.
