Sbruice Singh

Sbruice Singh is a researcher and the developer of AUDP-ECM, the error-correction engine of the ASPF Unified Device Protocol at Agriculture Service of Product Farm (ASPF).

Work

  • AUDP-ECM — error correction for agricultural sensors and farm telemetry.
  • AUDP-ECM for Python and AUDP-ECM for D — safe language bindings.
  • Agriculture research & innovation at ASPF — reliable farm telemetry and data integrity.

Areas of expertise

Error-correcting codes (Reed–Solomon, BCH), finite-field mathematics, agricultural telemetry and sensor networks, and safe systems software in Rust, Python and D.
